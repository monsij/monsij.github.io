Short Bio

Hello. I'm Monsij Biswal, a final year undergraduate at National Institute of Technology Durgapur pursuing my B. Tech in Electronics and Communication Engineering.

My broad area of interest is Wireless Communication, Millimeter Waves and Signal Processing. Recently , I have been investigating the application of neural networks in these domains.

I am familiar with programming in Python, C, C++, MATLAB. I implemented a custom training function for neural network during my internship with Prof. Stephan Pachnicke at Germany.

Besides work, I am a aviation enthusiast and an avid shutterbug.
